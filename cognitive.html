The Product

A two-capsule PM cognitive supplement. Taken before sleep. Positioned around overnight neurochemical replenishment — rebuilding the neurotransmitter substrate that a demanding day depletes.

"Feed your brain while you sleep."
Tagline Direction

This is not a sleep aid. It does not contain melatonin, valerian, or anything sedating in a meaningful sense. It is a brain nutrition product timed to leverage the sleep window — when consolidation, repair, and restoration are already happening. The positioning is biologically coherent and completely differentiated from what exists at retail.
